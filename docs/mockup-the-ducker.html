Drawn in code (this is what the plug-in would look like)

  • Borders, buttons and pills are one family: a brass rim round a dark face with a black keyline; the section panels have riveted corners; buttons have square corners like the gear teeth, pills stay round. The knob gear has square teeth (14, flat tops, straight sides).
  • Knobs: drag up or down (Shift = fine), mouse wheel, double-click resets. Mix, Smooth, Offset as in Ingot's Ducker today. The big DUCK knob is Ingot's Depth (how far the sound drops).
  • Curve: the 13 factory shapes from Ingot's Ducker. Behind the curve, the sound coming in (faint) and going out (yellow) is drawn as a waveform mirrored around the middle line, folded onto one pass, so the ducking shows as the yellow pinching in. It scales itself so the loudest recent part fills most of the height (quiet tracks still show their shape).
  • The preset pill opens factory and your saved presets. "+ Save current" saves the curve and knobs (kept in this browser only).
  • Duck Meter: IN and OUT level, with how much it is ducking in dB underneath. Left/right gain reduction would show two identical bars, since both sides duck together.
  • The title bar's size box sets the window to 75 to 150%.
  • Trigger: Beat repeats the curve with the host's tempo. Audio (sidechain input) and MIDI (notes in) start the curve again on every hit or note; the 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 buttons become its Length, and after one pass it holds the curve's end value until the next hit. The lamp flashes on each hit. The status line under the buttons says what it is listening to; after 2 bars of play with nothing arriving it turns orange and says where to route a source in Live (try "Source routed: no" above). Hit detection is automatic (no threshold). MIDI reacts to the start of each note only; note length does nothing. In Audio and MIDI the Offset knob becomes Delay (0 to 100 ms after each hit). This page fakes a kick part with an off-beat hit (Audio) and an uneven MIDI pattern.
